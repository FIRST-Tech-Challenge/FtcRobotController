## Ground rules decided with the team

- **Solo.** Plan as if we are the only robot on the field. Partner-aware versions come later.
- **Hive tipping.** Each hive starts with 3 nectar in its raised cell, so the first 4 pollen tip it. After that a cell needs 8 pollen to tip back. Only the raised cell scores; low-goal shooting is out of scope.
- **Start state.** Red's audience cell starts raised; blue's far cell starts raised (Event Field Setup Guide §11.1, also in Zenith's field model).
- **Aiming.** Localize from the raised hive tags, then aim from odometry with yaw from the field pose and hood/RPM from the calibrated distance table, the same as competition TeleOp.
- **Blue = point mirror.** The field is point-symmetric. Blue Far runs Red Audience's routine and Blue Audience runs Red Far's.
- **Intake is on the front**, the end the robot "faces".
- **Depollinator** deploys on the way into a flower and goes home as soon as loading is full or times out.
- **Red Far stays as described.** Its first volley goes at the far cell at about 5 s, even though solo that cell is still lowered. It scores once a partner tips the hive first.
- **Red Audience ends on the far side.** The original ending (last volley from the red audience corner) would hit the lowered audience cell in a solo run. The last volley therefore goes into the far cell from the far side, which brings it to 8 and tips the hive back so the audience cell is up for TeleOp.

## Implementation work

1. **Tag-localized aiming in autonomous.** `BioBuzzRobot` does not construct the Limelight today, and `ShotCoordinator` runs in `AUTONOMOUS_ODOMETRY`. Reuse TeleOp's acquisition: three consistent frames of raised tags, accepted only near the authored start pose. Seed Pinpoint with it and keep TeleOp's periodic correction (at most one every 0.5 s, 35% gain, 0.5 m jump rejection). Keep `LimelightObservation` as the single frame conversion point, and keep rejecting lowered or moving cells. Cover both alliances in tests.
   - Red Audience start: at turret home the camera looks +Y, about 50–60° away from the red audience tags (34–37). Acquisition has to pre-rotate the turret to the volley-1 yaw (≈ −36°).
   - Red Far start: red's far cell (tags 30–33) is lowered at the start and must not be used. The raised blue far tags (42–45) are about 21–33° off the camera axis at volley-1 yaw, at the edge of the field of view. Plan a small turret sweep or accept the authored start pose until a tag is seen.
   - Decide whether homing and acquisition run in INIT (as TeleOp does) or after START. Check the game manual's rules on mechanism motion during INIT first. The drafts home after START (2 s bound).
2. **Shot table.** Calibrate hood/RPM over at least 34–60 in (the five volleys above sit at about 35.6, 37.6, 48 and 59.5 in) with **Calibrate: Hive Shot Table**. Then set the shot calibration as verified so `shotReady` can feed.
3. **Blue wrappers.** Generated wrappers use `new BioBuzzRobot()`, which takes the file's alliance and never mirrors. Add two hand-written Competition-group autos that load `red-audience` and `red-far` with `new BioBuzzRobot(TurretSubsystem.Alliance.BLUE)` (the `CompetitionAutonomous` pattern). Ivy then mirrors every pose once, and `aim(frame=AUTHORED)` swaps AUDIENCE/FAR. Each should preselect the matching competition TeleOp.
4. **Deploy.** Run `npm run zenith:deploy`, then build (`:TeamCode:testDebugUnitTest :TeamCode:assembleDebug`) and `.\scripts\check-zenith-apk.ps1`. Until then, `npm run zenith:check` reports the two new sources as not deployed.

## Measure before trusting these routes

- The exact start spots: x along the wall for both starts. Red Audience must not touch the garden; Red Far must not touch flower1 and must stay on red's half.
- Robot footprint and the deployed depollinator envelope. Zenith still uses the 18 × 18 in placeholder; Red Far's turn off the wall passes about 1 in from flower1's foot.
- The flower approach: which direction, how far, and whether the depollinator needs a dwell or wiggle to release pollen.
- Garden sweep end x, and whether the intake picks up pollen 1.4 in off the wall with the robot touching it.
- Real `turretHome`, spin-up and 4-ball feed times. `shoot` is timed (2.5 s), not counted.
- Pedro tuning on V1 (the current gains are PORTED from master) and whether the 0.35 path-speed cap can rise. Red Audience needs the margin.
