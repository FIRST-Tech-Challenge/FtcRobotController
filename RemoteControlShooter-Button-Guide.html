Remote Control Button Guide

Only controls used by RemoteControl.java and RockinBot.java are shown.

Gamepad 1 Controls

Each information block connects related functions to the exact controls marked by red dots.

Starting Settings

Shooter power: 76%
Shooter P value: 60
Intake: Reverse at full power
Trigger motor: Stopped
Gamepad 2 left bumper: Decrease shooter P by 2
Gamepad 2 right bumper: Increase shooter P by 2

Important Control Details

Bumpers, Triangle, and D-pad: Each action occurs once per press, not continuously while held.
Triggers: The trigger motor runs only while a trigger is held and stops when both are released. If both are held, reverse wins.
Drive orientation: The intake side is the back of the robot; the opposite side is the front.
Lumberjack: The servo releases and can move freely after Remote Control stops.
No park mode: The wheel controls always use their normal full range.
